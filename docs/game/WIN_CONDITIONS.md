# Win Conditions Architecture

## Overview
A match concludes when any of the following win conditions are met:
1. **PRIZES_TAKEN**: A player takes all of their Prize cards.
2. **NO_POKEMON_IN_PLAY**: An opponent's Active Pokémon is knocked out and they have no Pokémon remaining on their Bench to promote.
3. **DECK_OUT**: A player is required to draw a card from an empty deck at the start of their turn or draw phase.
