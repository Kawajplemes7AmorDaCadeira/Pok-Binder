# Trainers Architecture (Items, Supporters, Stadiums, Tools)

## Overview
Trainer cards provide strategic advantages during the Main Phase:
1. **Items**: Played freely during the Main Phase without per-turn numerical limits.
2. **Supporters**: Limited to a maximum of 1 per turn (`supporterUsedThisTurn`).
3. **Stadiums**: Shared field modification cards. Playing a new Stadium replaces and discards the previous one.
4. **Pokémon Tools**: Attached to specific Pokémon in play to grant ongoing benefits (1 per Pokémon).
